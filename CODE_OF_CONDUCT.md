# Code of conduct

## Community standard

Participation in this repository must remain respectful, professional, and focused on improving the Jointl public plugin package.

Acceptable participation includes:

- constructive technical feedback;
- clear, reproducible issue reports;
- respect for different backgrounds and experience levels;
- responsible handling of security and privacy concerns; and
- compliance with applicable laws and platform policies.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, spam, exposure of private information, and publication of credentials or customer data are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@join.tl`. Jointl maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when required to protect the community, users, or service.
