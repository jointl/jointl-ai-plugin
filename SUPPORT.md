# Jointl support

## Connection and access help

If Jointl cannot connect or returns no visible records:

1. Confirm that you signed in with the intended Jointl account.
2. Confirm that the account belongs to the intended workspace.
3. Confirm that your Jointl role includes the Third-Party Apps permission and access to the requested records.
4. Reconnect Jointl from Claude, ChatGPT, or Codex.
5. Retry the request after the connection completes.

Jointl always follows your current role and permissions. Reconnecting does not grant additional access.

## Contact support

Email `support@join.tl` for:

- OAuth connection or revocation problems;
- account, workspace, role, or permission questions;
- hosted MCP availability;
- billing or plan questions; and
- privacy or customer-data requests.

Include the client name, approximate time, Jointl workspace name, and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys, candidate answers, employee records, reference content, or other sensitive data by email.

Use the repository's **Issues** tab for reproducible, non-sensitive problems with the public plugin instructions or packaged skills.

## Security reports

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish suspected vulnerabilities in a GitHub issue.
